This policy explains when you can get Telegram Stars back. Refunds go back as Stars to the Telegram account that paid. Telegram does not let us refund in cash or to another account.

Community memberships

The creator named on the offer sells the membership. MemberOS handles refund requests for MemberOS-billed communities. We will refund a payment if:

  • you were charged twice for the same community;
  • you paid but could not get into the community because of a problem with MemberOS or the creator's setup, and it was not fixed within 72 hours of you telling us;
  • the community was closed or removed during a period you had paid for; or
  • the creator agrees to a refund, or the law requires one.

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and we do not refund the unused part of a period unless one of the reasons above applies.

How to ask: open the community in MemberOS and tap Need help?, or send /paysupport to our bot. Tell us the payment date and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 3 business days. If we approve, the refund is sent through Telegram, your membership ends straight away, and its renewal is turned off.

For Telegram channel subscriptions, Telegram processes the payment and pays the creator directly. Contact the creator's support contact shown on the offer, and if that does not resolve it, use /paysupport so we can help you reach them.

MemberOS Pro for creators

  • You can cancel Pro at any time in MemberOS > Settings. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If you ask within 7 days of your first Pro payment, we will refund it in full.
  • We always refund duplicate Pro charges and charges made after you cancelled.
  • Otherwise we do not refund the unused part of a period, unless the law requires it.
  • A refunded Pro period ends straight away. Your communities and data are not affected; your account returns to Free limits.

Email {{.Operator.Email}} or send /paysupport to our bot.

If we cannot agree

If you believe a legitimate refund was refused, Telegram lets you report the transaction: find its ID in Telegram under Settings > My Stars and follow Telegram's instructions. This does not affect any rights you have under consumer law.
