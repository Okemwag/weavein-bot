This policy explains what information MemberOS collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean {{.Operator.LegalName}}, which operates MemberOS. "Creator" means a person or business that uses MemberOS to run a paid Telegram community. "Member" means someone who joins or pays for one of those communities.

Our role

For creator accounts, our own website, and the security of the service, we decide how information is used and are responsible for it. For a creator's community members, we process information on the creator's behalf so they can sell memberships and control access. The creator decides who may join their community and is the seller of the membership. Creators are responsible for their own use of member information, including any notes they write.

What we collect

  • Telegram account details. Your numeric Telegram user ID, first name and username, which Telegram shares when you open MemberOS or interact with our bot. We do not receive your phone number, contacts or messages in other chats.
  • Membership and payment records. The communities and plans you join, subscription status, renewal and paid-through dates, amounts in Telegram Stars, Telegram payment reference IDs, refunds, and cancellations. Telegram, Apple, Google or Fragment process the purchase of Stars; we never receive card or bank details.
  • Access records. Join requests, and whether Telegram reports you as a member of a connected community, so we can let paying members in and remove members whose access has ended.
  • Things you send us. Support requests, refund requests, commands you send to our bot, and your reminder preferences.
  • Creator information. Workspace and display names, support contact, community titles, descriptions, rules and plans, private notes creators add about their members, and records of creator and operator actions.
  • Website enquiries. The name, email address, community link and message you send through our contact form.
  • Technical information. Each request is logged with its method, path, response status, duration and a random request ID. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit abuse and is not written to our logs. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and the Mini App keeps its sign-in token only in memory.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: verify your Telegram identity, take and confirm payments, grant and remove access, and send the messages you need about your membership. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you or the creator.
  • To give creators the records they need to run their community, such as their member list, payments and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, investigate disputes and fix problems. We rely on our legitimate interest in a safe, working service.
  • To send renewal reminders, which you can turn off at any time in the Mini App.
  • To keep accounting, tax and payment records and to meet legal requests, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Removing a member when their paid period ends or is refunded follows the membership terms they accepted.

Who can see it

  • The creator of each community you join sees your Telegram name, username and ID, your membership and payment history in their community, your support requests, and their own notes. They do not see your activity in other creators' communities.
  • Telegram receives the information needed to issue invoices, process Stars payments and refunds, and manage chat membership. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to what it does.
  • Service providers that host our servers, databases and backups, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for us.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or when needed to protect people, investigate fraud, or enforce our terms.
  • A successor if MemberOS is reorganised, merged or sold, who must continue to protect your information under this policy.

How long we keep it

  • Raw Telegram updates: 90 days after processing, or 180 days if they needed review.
  • The text of bot messages we sent you: 90 days.
  • Resolved support requests: the description and resolution are removed 24 months after resolution.
  • Website enquiries: 12 months after we resolve them, and never more than 24 months.
  • Telegram profile details of people who opened MemberOS but never joined, paid, created a community or asked for help: 12 months after their last visit.
  • Payment, refund, subscription and audit records: as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and disputes, normally up to seven years.
  • Creator account and community information: while the account is active. When a creator asks us to delete their workspace, we remove what we are not required to keep.

Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted information may remain in a backup for a short time before it expires.

Where it is stored

Our servers or service providers may be in a different country from you. When we transfer personal information across borders, we use safeguards required by applicable law, such as contractual protections, and we transfer only what is needed.

Security

We verify every Telegram sign-in and webhook, encrypt traffic in transit, restrict operator access, record operator actions, and keep payment data to the minimum Telegram provides. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, the EU and UK GDPR, and similar laws, you may have the right to:

  • be told what we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have information deleted, where we are not required to keep it;
  • object to or restrict some uses, and withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • receive your information in a portable format; and
  • complain to a data protection authority, such as Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner or the authority where you live.

To use these rights, email {{.Operator.Email}} from an address we can link to your request and include your Telegram username. We may ask you to confirm the request through our bot so we know it is you. We reply within 7 days where Kenyan law applies, and otherwise within 30 days. Requests about a community's member list may be passed to that creator, because they control it.

Children

MemberOS is not intended for anyone under 16, or under the minimum age to use Telegram or make purchases where they live. We do not knowingly collect information about children. If you believe a child has used MemberOS, contact us and we will delete their information where we can.

Changes

We will post any change here with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell creators and members through the bot or the Mini App before it takes effect.
