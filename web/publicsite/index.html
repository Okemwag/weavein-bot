Open the community

Live in three steps

  1. Connect your private channel

    Pick your channel in Telegram and add MemberOS as an admin. We check everything before you go live.

  2. Set your price

    Choose a monthly price in Stars. Add more plans whenever you like. Current members keep what they signed up for.

  3. Share your link

    Send your link anywhere. People pay inside Telegram and are approved automatically once payment is confirmed.

The busywork, handled

The right people get in

Join requests from paying members are approved automatically. Everyone else is pointed to your plans.

Renewals look after themselves

Telegram charges every 30 days. If a renewal runs late, members keep their access while it comes through.

Nobody slips through

When a membership ends or is refunded, that person is removed. If they pay again, they can come straight back.

Your existing members are safe

People already in your chat when you connect it are never removed automatically.

Help requests in one place

Members can ask for help from their membership page, and you see every request together.

Clear numbers

See active members, new joiners, cancellations and Stars earned, and export them whenever you need.

Simple for your members too

Everything happens inside Telegram, with no new account or password.

  • They see the price, seller and renewal terms before paying
  • They can see their renewal date at any time
  • They can cancel in two taps and keep what they paid for
  • They can ask for a refund or get help from the same page
